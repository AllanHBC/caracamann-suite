Privacy Policy

What the app is

CaracaMann Stream Suite runs only on the developer's own Windows computer. It is not sold, not distributed and not offered to the public. Its only user is the developer, Allan Caraça, who owns the YouTube channel the app uploads to.

How it uses YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube Data API v3 to:

  • upload videos (clips, Shorts and full broadcasts) to the developer's own channel;
  • set each video's title, description, chapters, privacy status and thumbnail;
  • read the view counts of the developer's own videos, to show which kinds of clips perform best.

It requests only the permissions needed for those actions. It does not read, collect or store data about other YouTube users, channels or viewers.

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Instagram and TikTok

The app also publishes the same clips to the developer's own accounts on two other platforms:

  • Instagram (through the Instagram API with Facebook Login): uploads a clip and publishes it as a Reel with its caption on the developer's own Instagram professional account.
  • TikTok (through the TikTok Content Posting API): uploads a clip to the developer's own TikTok inbox as a draft. The developer finishes and publishes it in the TikTok app.

On both platforms the app only sends videos and captions to the developer's own account. It does not read followers, comments, messages or any data about other users. Meta's and TikTok's handling of data is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Data the app stores

  • The Google, Meta and TikTok sign-in tokens are saved only on the user's computer, in %APPDATA%\CaracaMannSuite. Each token is sent only to the platform that issued it.
  • Video files, titles and descriptions stay on the user's computer until they are uploaded.
  • View counts are read on demand and shown on screen only. They are not saved.
  • The app has no website backend, no analytics, no cookies and no advertising.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account permissions page, in Facebook's Business Integrations settings, and in TikTok's Manage app permissions settings. Deleting the folder %APPDATA%\CaracaMannSuite removes every piece of data the app keeps.

Changes

If the app's use of YouTube data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
